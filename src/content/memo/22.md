[Debouncing and Throttling Explained Through Examples | CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/debouncing-throttling-explained-examples/)[^22-1]

- throttle: 함수가 호출되는 속도를 제한 (일정시간 동안)[^22-2]
- debounce: 추가 호출 없이 일정 시간이 경과할 때까지 함수 실행을 지연하거나 제한 (마지막 이벤트만)[^22-3]

[^22-1]: 검색 입력, 스크롤 이벤트, 크기 조정 이벤트 등 디바운싱과 스로틀링이 유용할 수 있는 사례들
[^22-2]: [Throttle | MDN Glossary](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Glossary/Throttle): *"it refers to slowing down a process such that an operation can only be performed at a certain rate."*
[^22-3]: [Debounce | MDN Glossary](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Glossary/Debounce): *"throttling enforces limits on continuous operations, while debouncing waits for invocations to stop for a specific time to consolidate many noisy invocations into one single invocation."*
